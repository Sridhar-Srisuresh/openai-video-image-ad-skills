# YouTube Thumbnail Skill

Generate a thumbnail designed for high click-through. Focus on clean subject visibility, punchy contrast, and immediate emotional signal.

## Workflow

1. Identify thumbnail category.
2. Choose formula: reaction shot, before/after split, contrast shock, real-vs-AI reveal, logo-led brand cue.
3. Combine the product and a strong emotional framing.
4. Generate image.
5. QA readability, contrast, and subject clarity.

## Example formulas

- **reaction shock**: subject mid-reaction, object in foreground, high contrast
- **before/after**: split layout with indicator labels
- **real-vs-AI**: same pose or product shown in two states
- **brand shock**: bold type, huge eye-catching color contrast

## Guardrails

- Keep text minimal and legible at small size.
- Prioritize subject focus and contrast over extraneous background details.
