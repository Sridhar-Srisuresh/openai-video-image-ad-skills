# Claymation Ad Skill

Generate a clay-style ad using a compact story arc and a consistent stop-motion aesthetic.

## Typical flow

1. Pick a 5–8 beat story arc.
2. Generate one key frame per beat.
3. Generative motion or local edit per beat.
4. Add narration or sound-bed.
5. Assemble with ffmpeg.
6. Final QA.

## Visual guidance

- Warm, tactile clay textures
- Slow, deliberate movement
- Character and props made of compact clay forms
- Playful but premium stop-motion feel

## Example brief

> Create a claymation product reveal with a warm, handcrafted texture, five beats, clear product shot, and a direct ending with a product CTA.
