# Video Ad Prompt Formulas

Reusable prompt templates for short-form video ads.

## UGC Testimonial (Person-to-Camera)

```
[DURATION]-second UGC testimonial video: person holding [PRODUCT], speaking directly to camera
about the benefit, natural kitchen lighting, handheld smartphone aesthetic, visible motion blur,
slightly off-center framing, authentic micro-expressions.

Beat structure:
  - Hook: immediate product reveal and eye contact (1-2s)
  - Benefit: person explains why [PRODUCT] works (4-6s)
  - Demo: hands show the product in use (optional, 2-3s)
  - CTA: final recommendation to camera (1-2s)

Voice-over: [DIALOGUE]
No subtitles, no text overlays. Aspect ratio: 9:16.
```

## Product Hero / Reveal

```
[DURATION]-second product hero video: dark void background, product enters frame with elemental
effects (mist, light rays, subtle smoke), slow 360-degree rotation, premium lighting, no person.
Narrative text appears as overlay describing the benefit.

No dialogue, no subtitles. Aspect ratio: 9:16.
```

## Talking-Head Feature Demo

```
[DURATION]-second feature walkthrough: founder or expert sits at desk, explains [FEATURE] of
[PRODUCT] with hand gestures. Product visible on screen or in hand. Clear articulation,
natural pacing, eye contact to camera.

Beat structure:
  - Intro: who is speaking (1s)
  - Problem: what problem does [FEATURE] solve (2-3s)
  - Solution: how [FEATURE] works with live demo (4-6s)
  - CTA: call to action (1-2s)

Voice-over: [DIALOGUE]
No subtitles. Aspect ratio: 16:9.
```

## Silent Motion Ad (B-Roll)

```
[DURATION]-second silent motion ad: [PRODUCT] on clean background with minimal animation.
Slow zoom, subtle pan, slight rotation. No dialogue, no narration.
Designed to play muted on social feeds.

Aspect ratio: 9:16.
```

---

**Guardrails:**
- Always include camera imperfections for UGC styles (motion blur, grain, slight overexposure)
- Include 3–4 motion cues for person-on-screen videos (head tilts, hand gestures, weight shifts)
- Specify dialogue separately and require approval before generation
- Keep dialogue between 15–30 words for 8–15 second clips
